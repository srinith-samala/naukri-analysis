# naukri-analysis

## In this data set, I've analyzed the naukri datasets, such as:
- the job role and total number of roles
- the industry  and which is in high demand
-  which City has the most number of active jobs
- where there is most job listing and other things
- This consists of 30,000 rows .

## Cleanning
 In this dataset, there were no null values and a duplicate value. There were some inconsistencies in the data. There was extra spacing in the data. I standardize the format of a name where I standardize it Such as IT-Software inconsistency, leading spaces in Job_Experience, 0-0 Years → Fresher



## Insights 
Does location affect the number of job postings in IT industry
Which are the most in-demand roles and what is the average experience needed
Which skills appear across the most different industries
Do certain industries require more experience than others
Which industries are hiring the most

1.I found that	Most IT jobs on Naukri are in Bengaluru and require 2 to 5 years of experience. This matters because It shows that tech hiring in India is not spread out. If you do not live in Bengaluru or have a few years of experience, it is much harder to find a job. The business should Create a special "Bengaluru Relocation" feature on the website that connects people from other cities to companies willing to help them move.
2.	I found that software developers are the most demanding jobs with an average experience of 3.2 years. It matters because companies are hiring mid-tier experienced people rather than freshers. Businesses should focus on having one specific page, like a Naukri lead, where software developers can join when we have more than 3 years of experience.
3.	I found that 47 job listings have no skills listed, and the technical skills such as C++, MySQL, JavaScript, and Python are more common across 12 industries. It matters because technical skills are the most demanding. A business should not allow a company to post a job listing without listing a single skill. It helps the job seekers to apply to the post that matters the specific skills rather than just applying randomly.
4.	I found that no industry requires a minimum job experience, and I can find that a call centre has the lowest average experience at 1.38 years, while the IT sector standard requires 4.58 years of average. Here, that matters because getting into a call centre job is quite easier than getting into an IT software job, which requires higher experience. the business should build a career pathway feature that recommends the next logical industry transition based on the user's current experience
5.	I Found that IT software has the most number of jobs, which is 10,798. It's a matter because it's the most in-demand industry. Business should focus on building a separate, specific page where only IT job listings are posted. Rather than mixing all the job listings, make it easier for job seekers to find relevant opportunities.
